# CYMONIA Sovereign World v2

## Cinematic world presentation

- Layered isometric terrain with procedural detail and depth sorting.
- Animated water, foliage, atmospheric particles, daylight and weather.
- Action-specific Citizen motion, selection feedback and follow camera.
- Detailed resources, structures and visible construction progress.
- RTS-style HUD, minimap travel, keyboard camera controls and fullscreen.
- Text-state and deterministic time hooks for browser verification.

This release replaces the dashboard-centric autonomous-economy prototype with a causally closed, persistent artificial-world substrate.

## Binding changes

- Fresh v2 Genesis: 100 Citizens, natural territory, finite physical endowment, no pre-created society.
- 1 real minute = 1 CYMONIA hour.
- Material conservation and provenance-bound objects.
- Biological needs, disease, genetics, reproduction, childhood, aging and permanent death.
- Memory, forgetting, beliefs, knowledge provenance and epistemic validation.
- Emergent signals/lexicons/grammar; no human language at Genesis.
- Generic claims, commitments, relationships and organizations instead of hard-coded government/company/religion systems.
- General material transformation, experimentation, design and construction without a fixed tech tree.
- Event-driven Workers AI cognition with a hard budget and no fabricated social fallback.
- Human-linked avatar with equal physical vulnerability and no privileged Earth knowledge.
- Durable Object SQLite single writer, alarms, sealed checkpoints and WebSocket live delivery.
- Game-only Observer UI with Society History and causal `WHY?`.
- The repository contains only the Sovereign World runtime; legacy economy and dashboard paths are removed.

See the v2 design specification and architecture documentation for the full contract.
